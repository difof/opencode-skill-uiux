- Use this implementation checklist when a complete WCAG 2.2 A/AA audit is requested; read only the relevant criteria for a focused fix, and consult each linked normative criterion for exact definitions, applicability, exceptions, and notes.
- Cover all 55 Level A/AA criteria in the pinned Recommendation, recording pass, fail, not applicable with a reason, or not tested for every criterion in scope; this checklist is an implementation aid, not a replacement technical specification or a conformance certificate.
- Assess whole pages and complete processes, accessibility-supported uses of technology, and non-interference before making conformance claims; evaluate third-party content and error states, and distinguish rendered, keyboard, assistive-technology, and automated evidence.

- **1.1.1 (A)** [Non-text Content](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#non-text-content): Inspect informative images, charts, controls, tests, sensory content, and CAPTCHA for equivalent alternatives or the applicable special case; hide purely decorative content from assistive technology.
- **1.2.1 (A)** [Audio-only and Video-only (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-only-and-video-only-prerecorded): Provide an equivalent alternative for prerecorded audio-only, and an alternative or audio track for prerecorded video-only; evaluate the clearly labelled text-alternative exception.
- **1.2.2 (A)** [Captions (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#captions-prerecorded): Check accurate synchronized captions for prerecorded audio in video, including meaningful sounds and speakers; apply the labelled media-alternative exception only when valid.
- **1.2.3 (A)** [Audio Description or Media Alternative (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-description-or-media-alternative-prerecorded): Check that prerecorded video information is available through audio description or a full media alternative, accounting for the labelled text-alternative exception.
- **1.2.4 (AA)** [Captions (Live)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#captions-live): Check captions for live audio in synchronized media; a transcript published afterward does not supply live captions.
- **1.2.5 (AA)** [Audio Description (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-description-prerecorded): Check audio description of important prerecorded visual information in synchronized media; the A-level transcript option alone does not satisfy this AA requirement.
- **1.3.1 (A)** [Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#info-and-relationships): Inspect headings, landmarks, lists, table headers, form labels, groups, and relationships in the accessibility tree rather than relying on visual appearance.
- **1.3.2 (A)** [Meaningful Sequence](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#meaningful-sequence): Read the DOM or accessibility-tree sequence without visual positioning and confirm it preserves meaning; check reordered columns and responsive layouts.
- **1.3.3 (A)** [Sensory Characteristics](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#sensory-characteristics): Make instructions understandable without relying solely on shape, color, size, position, orientation, or sound; name the relevant action or object.
- **1.3.4 (AA)** [Orientation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#orientation): Operate content in both orientations unless a specific orientation is essential to the activity; device convenience is not by itself an exception.
- **1.3.5 (AA)** [Identify Input Purpose](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#identify-input-purpose): For supported fields collecting the user's own information, expose the applicable input purpose programmatically, such as correct autocomplete tokens.
- **1.4.1 (A)** [Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#use-of-color): Ensure errors, selected states, chart series, instructions, and links do not convey required information through color alone.
- **1.4.2 (A)** [Audio Control](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-control): For automatic audio lasting over three seconds, provide pause/stop or an independent volume control; assess interfering content across the whole page.
- **1.4.3 (AA)** [Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#contrast-minimum): Measure actual composited text contrast at 4.5:1, or 3:1 for qualifying large text; inspect the defined inactive, incidental, decorative, and logo exceptions rather than assuming them.
- **1.4.4 (AA)** [Resize Text](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#resize-text): Resize text to 200% without lost content or functionality, applying the captions and images-of-text exceptions where relevant.
- **1.4.5 (AA)** [Images of Text](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#images-of-text): Use actual text when the technology can achieve the presentation; evaluate user-customizable or essential presentation exceptions, including logotypes.
- **1.4.10 (AA)** [Reflow](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#reflow): Check reflow without two-axis scrolling at 320 CSS px width for vertical scrolling, or 256 CSS px height for horizontal scrolling; keep genuinely two-dimensional exceptions local.
- **1.4.11 (AA)** [Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#non-text-contrast): Measure required visual control/state and graphic information at 3:1 against adjacent colors, considering inactive controls, unmodified browser controls, and essential graphics exceptions.
- **1.4.12 (AA)** [Text Spacing](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#text-spacing): Override line height to 1.5, paragraph spacing to 2, letter spacing to 0.12, and word spacing to 0.16 times the font size without losing content/functionality; apply script-specific exceptions.
- **1.4.13 (AA)** [Content on Hover or Focus](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#content-on-hover-or-focus): Test additional hover/focus content for dismissal without moving the pointer/focus when required, pointer entry without disappearing, and persistence until dismissed, invalid, or no longer triggered; check exact exceptions.
- **2.1.1 (A)** [Keyboard](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#keyboard): Complete every applicable action by keyboard without timing individual keystrokes; assess the underlying path-dependent-function exception, not merely the chosen input implementation.
- **2.1.2 (A)** [No Keyboard Trap](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#no-keyboard-trap): Enter and leave components using keyboard alone; document any nonstandard exit method and distinguish legitimate modal containment with an exit from a trap.
- **2.1.4 (A)** [Character Key Shortcuts](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#character-key-shortcuts): For character-only shortcuts, verify a disable/remap option or operation only when the relevant component has focus; protect ordinary typing and speech-input use.
- **2.2.1 (A)** [Timing Adjustable](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#timing-adjustable): For author-set time limits, test disable, adjustment to at least ten times the default, or warning with at least 20 seconds to extend at least ten times; verify real-time, essential, or over-20-hour exceptions.
- **2.2.2 (A)** [Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#pause-stop-hide): Check pause/stop/hide for automatically moving content lasting over five seconds alongside other content, and pause/stop/hide or frequency control for automatic updates; evaluate essential-activity exceptions.
- **2.3.1 (A)** [Three Flashes or Below Threshold](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#three-flashes-or-below-threshold): Avoid more than three flashes within one second unless below the defined general/red-flash thresholds; inspect all content that could interfere with page use.
- **2.4.1 (A)** [Bypass Blocks](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#bypass-blocks): Provide an operable way to bypass repeated blocks, such as a working skip link or appropriate structure; confirm the destination and subsequent focus order.
- **2.4.2 (A)** [Page Titled](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#page-titled): Check that each page title identifies its topic or purpose, including meaningful route and state changes where needed.
- **2.4.3 (A)** [Focus Order](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-order): Traverse focus in sequence and verify that it preserves meaning and operation across responsive layouts, inserted content, overlays, and removed controls.
- **2.4.4 (A)** [Link Purpose (In Context)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#link-purpose-in-context): Determine each link's purpose from its text or programmatic context, applying the generally ambiguous-purpose exception only when it actually fits.
- **2.4.5 (AA)** [Multiple Ways](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#multiple-ways): For pages in a collection, provide more than one way to locate them unless they are a step in, or result of, a process.
- **2.4.6 (AA)** [Headings and Labels](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#headings-and-labels): Check that headings and labels describe their topic or purpose; visual prominence and a nonempty accessible name are not enough.
- **2.4.7 (AA)** [Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-visible): Ensure keyboard users can identify the focused component through a visible indicator in a supported mode of operation.
- **2.4.11 (AA)** [Focus Not Obscured (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-not-obscured-minimum): Focus every control near sticky or overlay content and ensure author-created content does not entirely hide it; account for the documented user-configurable and user-opened-content notes.
- **2.5.1 (A)** [Pointer Gestures](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#pointer-gestures): Offer a single-pointer method without a path-based gesture for multipoint/path gesture functions unless the gesture is essential.
- **2.5.2 (A)** [Pointer Cancellation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#pointer-cancellation): Check pointer actions for up-event activation with abort/undo, reversal on release, or a valid essential down-event exception; avoid accidental commitment on pointer-down.
- **2.5.3 (A)** [Label in Name](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#label-in-name): Ensure an accessible name contains the visible text label, preferably at its start, so speech users can activate what they see.
- **2.5.4 (A)** [Motion Actuation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#motion-actuation): For device/user-motion actions, offer a UI alternative and the ability to disable motion activation, unless the supported-interface or essential-function exception applies.
- **2.5.7 (AA)** [Dragging Movements](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#dragging-movements): Offer a single-pointer alternative without dragging for drag functions, except essential dragging or an unmodified browser-provided behavior; keyboard support alone is insufficient.
- **2.5.8 (AA)** [Target Size (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#target-size-minimum): Use targets at least 24 by 24 CSS px or a valid exception; for undersized-target spacing, test non-overlap of centered 24 px circles with other targets/circles, and review equivalent, inline, browser, and essential exceptions.
- **3.1.1 (A)** [Language of Page](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#language-of-page): Expose the page's default human language programmatically and confirm it matches the actual primary content.
- **3.1.2 (AA)** [Language of Parts](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#language-of-parts): Expose language changes for passages/phrases, respecting exceptions for names, technical terms, indeterminate words, and words adopted into surrounding language.
- **3.2.1 (A)** [On Focus](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#on-focus): Move focus without unexpectedly submitting, navigating, opening a new context, or otherwise changing context.
- **3.2.2 (A)** [On Input](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#on-input): Change field/control values without an unexpected context change, unless the behavior was explained before the control was used.
- **3.2.3 (AA)** [Consistent Navigation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#consistent-navigation): Keep repeated navigation in the same relative order across pages unless the user initiates the change.
- **3.2.4 (AA)** [Consistent Identification](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#consistent-identification): Identify controls with equivalent functionality consistently across the page set, including their names, icons, and terminology.
- **3.2.6 (A)** [Consistent Help](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#consistent-help): When repeated help mechanisms exist, keep their relative order consistent for the same page variation unless the user changes it; this criterion does not require inventing a new help channel.
- **3.3.1 (A)** [Error Identification](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#error-identification): Identify automatically detected input errors in text and identify the affected item; color and a generic failed-submit toast alone are insufficient.
- **3.3.2 (A)** [Labels or Instructions](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#labels-or-instructions): Provide the labels or instructions people need to enter input correctly, including consequential formats, units, and constraints.
- **3.3.3 (AA)** [Error Suggestion](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#error-suggestion): Offer known correction suggestions for detected errors unless doing so compromises the content's security or purpose.
- **3.3.4 (AA)** [Error Prevention (Legal, Financial, Data)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#error-prevention-legal-financial-data): For legal commitments, financial transactions, stored user-data changes/deletion, and test responses, provide reversal, input checking with correction, or review/confirmation/correction before completion.
- **3.3.7 (A)** [Redundant Entry](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#redundant-entry): Populate or offer selection of information needed again in the same process; evaluate essential repetition, security, and no-longer-valid-information exceptions.
- **3.3.8 (AA)** [Accessible Authentication (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#accessible-authentication-minimum): Inspect every authentication step for memory/puzzle tests and provide an allowed alternative or assistance, or apply the object-recognition/personal-content exception correctly; support password managers and paste.
- **4.1.2 (A)** [Name, Role, Value](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#name-role-value): Inspect every control's programmatic name, role, settable states/properties/values, and change notifications; custom behavior must expose the same essential contract as native controls.
- **4.1.3 (AA)** [Status Messages](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#status-messages): Expose relevant status messages through suitable roles/properties so assistive technologies can announce them without moving focus; avoid indiscriminate live-region noise.
- WCAG 2.2 removed 4.1.1 Parsing; do not count it as a current A/AA criterion, while still fixing malformed markup when it causes other failures or violates the project's compatibility requirements.
